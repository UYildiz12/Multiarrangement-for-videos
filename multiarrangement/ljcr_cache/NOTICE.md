# Source and licence

The covering designs in this folder (`v{v}_k{k}_t2.txt`: one block per line, points numbered 0 to v-1) were taken
from the La Jolla Coverings Repository, maintained by Daniel M. Gordon, with improvements contributed by many
authors, before its final release. That release, version 1.2, is archived on Zenodo (doi:10.5281/zenodo.19735294)
under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and lists the contributors and methods of each cover in
`coverdata.json`. In 31 settings the release holds a smaller cover than this folder; the schedule portfolio in
`../frontier_cache` holds a cover at least that small in each of them.

Please cite: Gordon, D. M. La Jolla Coverings Repository, version 1.2. Zenodo (2026), doi:10.5281/zenodo.19735294.
