# Sources

`index.json` records the source of every schedule in this folder. Schedules taken from the La Jolla Coverings
Repository (final release v1.2, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.19735294) are used under CC BY 4.0; see
`../ljcr_cache/NOTICE.md`. Schedules from coveringrepository.com name their author and download in their source
field. All other schedules were constructed or rebalanced for this package.
